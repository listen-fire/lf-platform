From now on, when our website's enquiry form sends us an enquiry, add it to the Enquiries table of our Events base in Airtable with its enquiry id, name, email, event date and number of guests. If the email belongs to one of our clients in the Clients table, set Assigned To to that client's Account Manager; otherwise assign it to Dana. Set Priority to Urgent if the event is less than 14 days after the enquiry was sent, to High if it is for more than 150 guests, and to Normal otherwise (Urgent wins when both apply). The form sometimes sends the same enquiry twice, so keep one row per enquiry id.

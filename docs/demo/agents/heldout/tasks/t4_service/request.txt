Every morning at 7:00 UTC, check the Equipment table of our Facilities base in Airtable. For each piece of equipment whose Next Service date is in the next 7 days or has already passed, add a job to the Service Jobs table with the equipment's name, its Next Service date as the Due date, and Status "To do". Skip anything marked Retired. Equipment that already has a job that isn't Done must not get another one, and leave existing jobs as they are: the team books and closes them by hand.

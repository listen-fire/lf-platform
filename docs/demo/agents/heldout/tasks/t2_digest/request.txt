Every Monday at 9:00 UTC, add a digest of our open support tickets to the Digests table of our Support base in Airtable. A ticket in the Tickets table is open unless its Status is Done. The digest row has the date, the number of open tickets, and a breakdown with one line per assignee giving how many open tickets they have, like "Priya: 3"; open tickets nobody is assigned to get a line of their own, like "Unassigned: 2".
